# Chapter Zero · Logo usage guide

## Supplied identity
Preserve the supplied PNG artwork; do not redraw, retype, auto-trace, or rearrange it. Original vectors and transparent/monochrome masters were not supplied. Several PNGs have a solid background and built-in canvas margins. Measure the visible artwork, not the file edge. The website crops unused canvas for inspection only; downloads retain original files.

## Choose a signature
- Horizontal light: cream headers, landscape layouts, and identity signatures.
- Horizontal dark: Deep Spine panels; keep the distinct lighter and simplified mark as supplied.
- Primary stacked: portrait covers and generous opening slides.
- Standalone mark: only when the name is established nearby.
- App icon: profile previews; platform submissions need appropriate exports.

## Clear space
X is the visible orange zero’s stroke width. Leave at least 2X on all sides of a lockup including the tagline, and 1X around the standalone mark. Keep typography, rules, image subjects, partner logos, and page edges out of this zone. The online diagrams are schematic, not measured vector masters.

## Working minimums
Measured across visible artwork: horizontal with tagline 320 CSS px / 65mm; stacked with tagline 240px / 50mm; detailed mark 64px / 15mm; simplified app artwork 32px for previews only. These are conservative starting thresholds, not validated master specifications. Proof final output. Never shrink an illegible tagline or manually erase it from a raster. Obtain approved responsive variants for smaller uses.

## Backgrounds
Use the light asset on matching Paper Cream and dark asset on Deep Spine. On photographs or third-party colors, use an intact approved solid panel with clear space inside it. Avoid mismatched background patches. Logos over busy or low-contrast imagery are prohibited.

## Misuse
Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects or outlines, cut off artwork, separate its parts, retype the wordmark, replace letters with the mark, or place it directly on busy fields. Intentional misuse examples online are demonstrations only, never alternate assets.

## Placement and partnerships
Use one main signature per view. Default to top-left navigation/documents or a modest editorial footer. Center the stacked signature only in a roomy composition. Keep partner identities separate, honor both clear spaces, and balance optical prominence. Never imply unapproved endorsement or merge marks.

## Accessibility and production
A linked logo needs a useful accessible name such as “Chapter Zero home”. Decorative duplicate logos can have empty alt text. Proof legibility, effective print resolution, and background compatibility. Use original vector masters for large signage once obtained. Production monochrome, transparent, and tiny-size variants require original artwork work.
